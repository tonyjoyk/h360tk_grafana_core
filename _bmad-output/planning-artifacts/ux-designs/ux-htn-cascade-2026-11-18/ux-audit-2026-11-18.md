# UX audit: cascade card

## Summary

The card fits two of three columns, but it is hard to read. The treatment cascade mixes two kinds of percent. The screening cascade does not show the step rate in a clear way. The copy is too short, and the two charts do not line up.

## Findings

| # | Area | Finding | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Treatment, copy | The context line ("Oct 2026 · 250,740 estimated with hypertension") does not say who is counted. | High | Use one sentence: "Estimated adults (15+) in {place} with hypertension who are registered, under care and controlled, as of Oct 2026." |
| T2 | Treatment, copy | The labels are too short: "Estimated", "Registered", "Under care". | Medium | Use the reference labels: "Estimated people with hypertension", "Cumulative registered patients", "Patients under care", "Patients with BP controlled". |
| T3 | Treatment, numbers | The grey lines ("91% of registered") use a different denominator than the bar percent. Two denominators confuse the reader. | High | Remove the grey lines. Every percent is a share of the first bar. |
| T4 | Treatment, hierarchy | The percents are small (12 px), and the counts are colored. | Medium | Show a large colored percent above each bar. Show the count in black and the label in grey. |
| T5 | Both, scanning | Nothing links a bar to its count and label. | Low | Add thin column lines, as in the reference. |
| S1 | Screening, numbers | The step rate ("9% of screened") and the target progress ("87% of year target") look the same. | High | Show the step rate as a grey line: "9% of screened" (no arrow, owner 2026-11-18). Show target progress in the target color. |
| S2 | Screening, numbers | The diagnosed percent (0.9%) is under the count, not above the bar. | Medium | Always show the percent above the bar. It sits on a white background over the target line. |
| S3 | Screening, numbers | "2.1M" breaks the exact-count pattern. | Low | Show exact counts. With step 4 hidden there is room. |
| S4 | Screening, copy | "Eligible", "Raised BP" and "Diagnosed" are too short. | Medium | Use "Adults eligible for screening", "Raised BP (140/90 or higher)" and "Newly diagnosed with hypertension". |
| L1 | Layout | The two subtitles have different heights, so the charts do not line up. | Medium | Give both subtitles the same two-line height. |
| L2 | Layout | The labels are tight, but there is empty space at the bottom of the card. | Low | Use the space for the labels. Keep the chart at 120 px. |

## Answers to the owner's questions

- **Is the step rate clear?** No. It is in the same grey line style as the target progress. Fix S1.
- **Is the copy clear and descriptive?** No. The context lines and labels are too short. Fixes T1, T2 and S4.
- **Is the spacing correct?** Partly. The charts do not line up, and the text is tight. Fixes L1 and L2.
