Patients with hypertension (I10), grouped by the quarter they registered. Each bar is their outcome in the next quarter, after that quarter has ended. Controlled means systolic under 140 and diastolic under 90. Uncontrolled means systolic 140 or higher, or diastolic 90 or higher. Visited, no BP means a visit in that quarter without a complete blood pressure. Missed visit means no visit in that quarter. The four outcomes add up to the cohort. The latest visit in the quarter is the one that counts. Dead patients stay in the cohort. The five most recent closed quarters are shown, newest at the top.
